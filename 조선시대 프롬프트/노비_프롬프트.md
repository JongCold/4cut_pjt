# 조선시대 캐릭터 변환 프롬프트: 노비 (Commoner / Servant)

조선시대 서민 또는 노비의 소박하고 고단한 삶의 모습을 담은 실사풍 인물 변환(Image-to-Image) GPT/AI 이미지 생성 프롬프트입니다.  
**1인뿐만 아니라 2인 이상 다수의 인원이 함께 촬영해도 한 사람도 현대 옷으로 남지 않고, 모든 인물이 거친 삼베/무명 한복과 이마 흰 머리띠, 로우번(똥머리)을 착용한 채 100% 개별 변환**되도록 최적화되었습니다.

---

## 📌 프롬프트 원문 (Prompt)

```text
[Core Requirements]

Recreate this as ONE cohesive, naturally rendered photograph — not a composite or overlay. The people, clothing, and background must all share the same lighting, shadows, and photographic depth, as if photographed together in a single shot.
Preserve the exact pose, expression, body orientation, and position of every person in the photo.
Preserve each person's unique biological facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, nose, mouth, and skin tone.

CRITICAL MULTI-PERSON TRANSFORMATION & INDEPENDENT SUBJECT RULE:
- Recognize and treat EVERY SINGLE PERSON visible in the input image as an independent primary subject.
- Do NOT add, remove, merge, duplicate, or omit anyone. Preserve the exact number of people from the input image.
- ABSOLUTELY NO PERSON SHOULD BE LEFT IN MODERN CLOTHING: Every single person in the photo MUST be completely transformed into worn, humble traditional Korean commoner/servant (nobi) hanbok. Under no circumstances should any person remain in modern clothing (such as modern black tank tops, sleeveless shirts, t-shirts, straps, or casual wear).
- SERVANT COSTUMES FOR MULTIPLE PEOPLE:
  * All subjects (male and female) wear coarse, worn, plain white or off-white cotton/hemp fabric clothes with visible wrinkles, faded spots, and frayed edges (simple jeogori top with baji pants or humble skirt).
  * Tie a plain white cloth headband (meoritti) around the forehead, sitting naturally with realistic tension and shadow.
  * Restyle the hair into a neat low bun (ttongmeori) at the nape of the neck.

Do not add, remove, or alter any body parts (hands, arms, fingers, etc.) that are not clearly visible in the original photo. If a hand or arm is cropped out of frame in the original photo, do not invent or generate one.

[Layout]

Output image aspect ratio: 4:5 (width:height).
Fill the entire 4:5 frame with the scene (no extra margin, border, or blank space).

[Background & Environment]

Setting: a traditional Korean thatched-roof house (chogajip) — straw roof, mud-and-wood walls, a simple dirt yard, wooden fence, and rustic rural surroundings.
Include subtle period-appropriate elements such as earthenware jars (hangari), a well, straw mats, or firewood to reinforce a modest, rural household setting.

[Style & Atmosphere]

Mood: humble, weary, modest, and grounded — reflecting a hardworking life.
Lighting: a single consistent light source (soft, slightly muted natural daylight, warm but subdued) illuminating all subjects and background together — no mismatched lighting or flat cutout look.
Render style: photorealistic, 8k resolution, real photography look (not illustration or anime style), as if shot in one take, not edited afterward.
Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain.

[Strict Negative Constraints]

No modern clothing on any person, no modern tank tops, no sleeveless tops, no casual modern wear left untransformed.
No added text, watermark, or logo.
No composite/cutout/pasted-on look — the final image must look like a single, real photograph taken in one shot.
No mismatched lighting, harsh edges around any person, or flat/sticker-like appearance.
No changes to facial identities or expressions.
No invented or altered body parts (hands, arms, fingers) beyond what is visible in the original photo.
No panel borders, grid lines, or margins — this is a single standalone photo.
Clothing must look worn, coarse, and humble — not clean, new, or elegant.
Hair must be styled in a low bun (ttongmeori) with a white cloth headband — do not omit the headband.
```

## 💡 주요 특징 및 설정 요약
- **다인원 개별 최적화**: 2인 이상 촬영 시 전원 모두 거친 무명/삼베 저고리와 바지(치마), 흰 머리띠, 똥머리로 100% 변환. 현대 의상 완전 차단.
- **의상**: 낡고 기운 자국과 얼룩이 있는 거친 무명/삼베 저고리와 바지(또는 치마).
- **헤어/소품**: 목덜미에 단단히 묶은 똥머리(로우번) + 이마에 두른 흰색 천 머리띠.
- **배경**: 초가집(흙벽, 짚 지붕), 흙마당, 장독대, 우물, 장작더미.
- **분위기**: 소박하고 고단하며 진솔한 민초의 삶, 은은하고 따뜻하지만 절제된 자연광.
