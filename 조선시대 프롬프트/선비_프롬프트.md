# 조선시대 캐릭터 변환 프롬프트: 선비 (Joseon Scholar)

조선시대 양반가 사대부 및 유생의 단아하고 기품 있는 분위기로 인물 사진을 변환(Image-to-Image)하는 GPT/AI 이미지 생성 프롬프트입니다.  
**1인뿐만 아니라 2인 이상(커플, 친구, 가족) 다수의 인원이 함께 촬영해도 단 한 사람도 현대 옷에 방치되지 않고, 남성은 도포와 흑립(갓), 여성은 양반가 사대부 규수의 최고급 비단 한복으로 100% 개별 변환**되도록 최적화되었습니다.

---

## 📌 프롬프트 원문 (Prompt)

```text
[Core Requirements]

Recreate this as ONE cohesive, naturally rendered photograph — not a composite or overlay. The people, clothing, and background must all share the same lighting, shadows, and photographic depth, as if photographed together in a single shot.
Preserve the exact pose, expression, body orientation, and position of every person in the photo.
Preserve each person's unique biological facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, nose, mouth, and skin tone.

CRITICAL MULTI-PERSON TRANSFORMATION & INDEPENDENT SUBJECT RULE:
- Recognize and treat EVERY SINGLE PERSON visible in the input image as an independent primary subject.
- Do NOT add, remove, merge, duplicate, or omit anyone. Preserve the exact number of people from the input image.
- ABSOLUTELY NO PERSON SHOULD BE LEFT IN MODERN CLOTHING: Every single person in the photo MUST be completely transformed into traditional Joseon noble/aristocratic (Yangban) attire. Under no circumstances should any person remain in modern clothing (such as modern black tank tops, sleeveless shirts, t-shirts, modern straps, necklaces, or casual wear).
- GENDER & ROLE APPROPRIATE NOBLE COSTUMES FOR MULTIPLE PEOPLE:
  * Male subjects: Transformed into Joseon scholars wearing the flowing traditional scholar's outer robe (dopo) in ivory, white, or soft gray with a front tie sash (goreum), paired with the solid black horsehair hat (gat).
  * Female subjects: Transformed into noble Joseon ladies (Yangban aristocratic women) wearing elegant high-class traditional hanbok made of fine silk or ramie (graceful jeogori jacket and voluminous full skirt chima in tasteful pastel or jewel tones), with neatly styled traditional hair (braided daenggi or elegant bun with hairpin).
  * If multiple men or multiple women appear, dress all of them in appropriate noble Joseon aristocratic attire.

AUTHENTIC NOBLE HAIRSTYLE & GAT INTEGRATION:
- For subjects wearing the gat: All hair, front bangs, and side locks must be cleanly combed back, pulled up into an authentic topknot (sangtu), and tucked completely inside a black horsehair headband (manggeon) beneath the hat.
- Absolutely NO modern bangs falling onto the forehead, NO long sideburns, and NO messy wavy hair strands billowing out from beneath the brim of the gat. The forehead and eyebrows must be cleanly visible through the translucent black woven mesh.
- For female subjects: Hair neatly combed back and styled into an authentic elegant noblewoman's coiffure, with a clean hairline. Keep natural hair colors.

Do not add, remove, or alter any body parts (hands, arms, fingers, etc.) that are not clearly visible in the original photo. If a hand or arm is cropped out of frame in the original photo, do not invent or generate one.

[Layout]

Output image aspect ratio: 4:5 (width:height).
Fill the entire 4:5 frame with the scene (no extra margin, border, or blank space).

[Background & Environment]

Setting: a traditional Korean hanok house courtyard or wooden veranda (daecheong), with tiled roofs, wooden pillars, paper-latticed sliding doors (hanji windows), stone pathway, and scholarly atmosphere.
Include subtle traditional elements such as a stone pathway, a small garden, or an ink-and-brush writing desk nearby.

[Style & Atmosphere]

Mood: calm, refined, and scholarly.
Lighting: a single consistent light source (soft natural daylight, warm and gentle) illuminating all subjects and background together — no mismatched lighting or flat cutout look.
Render style: photorealistic, 8k resolution, real photography look (not illustration or anime style), as if shot in one take, not edited afterward.
Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain.

[Strict Negative Constraints]

No modern clothing on any person, no modern black tank tops, no sleeveless shirts, no casual clothing left untransformed.
No added text, watermark, or logo.
No composite/cutout/pasted-on look — the final image must look like a single, real photograph taken in one shot.
No mismatched lighting, harsh edges around any person, or flat/sticker-like appearance.
No changes to facial bone structure or identities.
No invented or altered body parts (hands, arms, fingers) beyond what is visible in the original photo.
No panel borders, grid lines, or margins — this is a single standalone photo.
The gat must be black only — no brown, tan, or other colors.
No modern floppy bangs hanging down from the gat, no loose hair spilling out over forehead or ears, no modern curtain bangs or wavy locks.
```

## 💡 주요 특징 및 설정 요약
- **다인원 개별 최적화**: 커플 또는 다인원 동반 촬영 시 여성이 현대 민소매 복장으로 남는 문제 완벽 해결. 남성=도포/흑립(갓), 여성=사대부 양반가 규수 최고급 실크 한복으로 100% 개별 변환.
- **의상**: 남성(백색/아이보리/연회색 도포 및 옷고름), 여성(우아한 저고리와 풍성한 비단 치마).
- **모자/헤어**: 남성(흑립 갓, 망건 속으로 앞머리/옆머리 완벽 정리), 여성(단아한 댕기머리 또는 쪽진머리/비녀).
- **배경**: 한옥 대청마루, 안마당, 돌길, 격자 창호문, 문갑/서안.
- **분위기**: 차분하고 정갈하며 기품 있는 사대부 학자풍, 부드러운 자연광.
