# 조선시대 캐릭터 변환 프롬프트: 보부상 (Traveling Peddler)

조선팔도를 누비는 보부상(장돌뱅이)의 활동적이고 소박한 모습을 담은 인물 변환(Image-to-Image) GPT/AI 이미지 생성 프롬프트입니다.  
**1인뿐만 아니라 2인 이상 다수의 인원이 함께 촬영해도 한 사람도 현대 옷으로 남지 않고, 모든 인물이 전통 보부상/장터 상인 복장과 패랭이 모자(양쪽 흰 솜뭉치 필수)를 착용한 채 100% 개별 변환**되도록 최적화되었습니다.

---

## 📌 프롬프트 원문 (Prompt)

```text
[Core Requirements]

Recreate this as ONE cohesive, naturally rendered photograph — not a composite or overlay. The people, clothing, and background must all share the same lighting, shadows, and photographic depth, as if photographed together in a single shot.
Preserve the exact pose, expression, body orientation, and position of every person in the photo.
Preserve each person's unique biological facial identity, bone structure, eye shape, nose, mouth, and skin tone.

CRITICAL MULTI-PERSON TRANSFORMATION & INDEPENDENT SUBJECT RULE:
- Recognize and treat EVERY SINGLE PERSON visible in the input image as an independent primary subject.
- Do NOT add, remove, merge, duplicate, or omit anyone. Preserve the exact number of people from the input image.
- ABSOLUTELY NO PERSON SHOULD BE LEFT IN MODERN CLOTHING: Every single person in the photo MUST be completely transformed into traditional Joseon peddler-merchant (bobusang) or market merchant attire. Under no circumstances should any person remain in modern clothing (such as modern black tank tops, sleeveless shirts, t-shirts, straps, or casual wear).
- PEDDLER COSTUMES FOR MULTIPLE PEOPLE:
  * All subjects (male and female) wear simple traditional merchant work clothes (jeogori jacket and baji pants or working chima skirt) in plain durable cotton, worn and practical looking.
  * Equipped with traditional merchant accessories: yellow bamboo straw hats (paengnyi) with white cotton tufts (somtteong) on both sides near the ears, and/or cloth bundle packs (bottjim) or wooden pack frames (jige) carried on the back.
  * Hair must be neatly tied back and secured beneath headbands or hats, so that NO modern bangs or messy hair spill over the eyes. Face and eyebrows clearly visible.

Do not add, remove, or alter any body parts (hands, arms, fingers, etc.) that are not clearly visible in the original photo. If a hand or arm is cropped out of frame in the original photo, do not invent or generate one.

[Layout]

Output image aspect ratio: 4:5 (width:height).
Fill the entire 4:5 frame with the scene (no extra margin, border, or blank space).

[Background & Environment]

Setting: a traditional Korean countryside road or old marketplace (jang-teo), with dirt paths, thatched-roof or tiled-roof houses, wooden market stalls, and rustic rural scenery.
Include subtle period-appropriate elements such as straw baskets, pottery, or other goods typical of a traditional Korean market.

[Style & Atmosphere]

Mood: warm, humble, and hardworking.
Lighting: a single consistent light source (soft natural daylight, warm and gentle) illuminating all subjects and background together — no mismatched lighting or flat cutout look.
Render style: photorealistic, 8k resolution, real photography look (not illustration or anime style), as if shot in one take, not edited afterward.
Shot on a full-frame camera, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain.

[Strict Negative Constraints]

No modern clothing on any person, no modern tank tops, no sleeveless shirts, no untransformed modern people, no modern straps.
No added text, watermark, or logo.
No composite/cutout/pasted-on look — the final image must look like a single, real photograph taken in one shot.
No mismatched lighting, harsh edges around any person, or flat/sticker-like appearance.
No changes to facial identities or expressions.
No invented or altered body parts (hands, arms, fingers) beyond what is visible in the original photo.
No panel borders, grid lines, or margins — this is a single standalone photo.
The straw hat must be yellow/straw-colored with a white cotton tuft on BOTH sides — do not omit either tuft.
No modern floppy bangs spilling over eyes, no messy modern hairstyle hanging outside the hat.
```

## 💡 주요 특징 및 설정 요약
- **다인원 개별 최적화**: 2인 이상 촬영 시 전원 모두 전통 보부상/장터 상인 무명 복장으로 변환. 현대 의류 완전 배제.
- **의상**: 실용적이고 튼튼한 무명 작업복(저고리, 바지 또는 일하는 치마).
- **모자**: 노란 패랭이 모자(양쪽 귀 부근에 흰색 목화솜 방울 2개 부착 필수).
- **소품**: 어깨에 멘 봇짐(Bottjim) 또는 지게(Jige), 줄 꼬임 및 하중 텐션 반영.
- **배경**: 전통 장터(주막, 목제 가판대) 또는 시골 흙길, 짚바구니, 토기.
- **분위기**: 근면하고 소박하며 활력 있는 분위기, 온화한 자연광.
